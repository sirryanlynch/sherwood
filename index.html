Disclosures

This assessment reduction filing service is not associated with any government agency. If you disagree with the assessed value of your property, you have the right to an informal assessment review, at no cost, by contacting the assessor's office directly. If you and the assessor cannot agree to the value of the property or if you do not wish to contact the assessor you can obtain and file an application for changed assessment with the county board of equalization or assessment appeals board on your own behalf. An appeals board has the authority to raise property values (but in no case higher than the Proposition 13 protected value) as well as to lower property values.

Questions

Before you check

What is an assessment appeal?

An application asking the county board to review the assessed value. In California the usual form is BOE-305-AH. Sherwood prepares it from the numbers you enter and, if you hire us, files it after you sign.

Can my taxes go up?

The board can raise, lower, or leave the assessment unchanged, and not above the Proposition 13 protected value. The launch-county instructions say that. Your result page repeats the rule from the county you picked. This is not legal advice. For legal questions, talk to an attorney.

What do I have to do?

You enter the address and the values, confirm the property, add condition notes if you have them, and sign. Sherwood does not sign the certification as you. You keep paying the tax bill. Filing does not pause it.

Do you take a percentage of the savings?

No. We file it is a flat $249 in Santa Cruz, county fee included. You keep 100% of any tax savings. Contingency fees stay off until a California property-tax attorney reviews them.

When am I charged?

The free check is $0. If you hire Sherwood to file, we'll save your card today. You won't be charged until the county confirms your appeal is filed. The annual monitor is $49/yr, on the same charge timing. Cancel before the county confirms the filing and there is no charge. The written terms are still a draft.
