Are you overpaying property tax?

Enter the property address and your email. On the next screen you add the assessed value from your tax bill and your own estimate of market value. We apply that county's deadline and fee from its adapter and show an estimate computed from your numbers.

We're launching county by county - we'll let you know when we're in your area.

Free. No payment. This demo does not pull comparable sales, and it does not send your email anywhere.

How it works

  1. Free check

    Address and email, then your assessed value and market-value estimate.

  2. Go, borderline, no-go, or off-season

    The result uses your numbers and that county's adapter. A person reviews borderline checks.

  3. Account, plan, intake

    You choose a plan, confirm the property, and add condition notes. You can upload photos in this browser.

  4. You sign. Then filing.

    You sign the certification. If you hired Sherwood to file, you also sign an agent authorization. Then you track the case.
