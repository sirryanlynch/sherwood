Disclosures

This assessment reduction filing service is not associated with any government agency. If you disagree with the assessed value of your property, you have the right to an informal assessment review, at no cost, by contacting the assessor's office directly. If you and the assessor cannot agree to the value of the property or if you do not wish to contact the assessor you can obtain and file an application for changed assessment with the county board of equalization or assessment appeals board on your own behalf. An appeals board has the authority to raise property values (but in no case higher than the Proposition 13 protected value) as well as to lower property values.

Privacy policy

This is a draft. It is not a finished policy and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Data this product can collect

  • Property address, when you type it into a check or a reminder.
  • Email address, when you ask us to save a result or send a reminder.
  • IP-based approximate location, used only to suggest a state and, when the lookup returns a county name, to suggest a county. The IP city is not shown in headlines.
  • The src and utm_* parameters on the first link that included them. They stay in this browser and are sent with a reminder or a saved result.
  • Signatures you type in the simulated signing step, stored with the appeal.
  • Payment via a processor. This demo does not collect a card number. A live payment would be handled by the processor, not typed into this page.

On the public demo, the check, the appeal, and a reminder are stored in this browser until a Firebase project is connected. After that, a reminder or a saved result is sent to that project.

Deletion requests

To ask for deletion, use the contact form and say what you want removed. This draft does not set a response time.
