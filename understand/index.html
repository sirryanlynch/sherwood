Disclosures

This assessment reduction filing service is not associated with any government agency. If you disagree with the assessed value of your property, you have the right to an informal assessment review, at no cost, by contacting the assessor's office directly. If you and the assessor cannot agree to the value of the property or if you do not wish to contact the assessor you can obtain and file an application for changed assessment with the county board of equalization or assessment appeals board on your own behalf. An appeals board has the authority to raise property values (but in no case higher than the Proposition 13 protected value) as well as to lower property values.

Your notice

What your assessment notice is telling you

A higher bill is not, by itself, a reason to appeal. This page separates the value from the rate. No county numbers are quoted here unless you run the check and your county file supplies them.

Value

Appealable. This is the assessed value an appeal can ask the county to review.

Rate and charges

Not appealable. Tax rates and voter-approved charges can rise while the value stays put.

Market value

What the home might sell for. You type this estimate on the check. Sherwood does not pull comparable sales for it.

Assessed value

The value on the assessment roll. An appeal asks the county to review this number.

Taxable value

The assessed value after exemptions the county has already applied. It is not the same number as your market estimate.

Your tax bill

Taxable value times the tax rate, plus voter-approved charges. The rate and those charges can rise while the assessed value stays put.

Why the bill can go up without your value going up

Tax rates and voter-approved charges sit on top of value. Only the value part is something an assessment appeal can ask the county to review. Sherwood files on value, not on the rate.

When an appeal might make sense

When your market estimate is below the assessed value, and the low savings estimate covers the filing fee. The check says so, or it says no action recommended.

When it doesn't

When the numbers don't clear the fee, or the bill went up because of the rate. That result is a real answer. You can ask for a reminder next year.
