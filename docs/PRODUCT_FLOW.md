# Sherwood product flow

This site is a static demo on GitHub Pages. The journey below is clickable. County facts are read from `data/counties/*.json`. Payments, email, DocuSign, and county e-file are simulated behind the adapters in `js/adapters/`. Nothing in those adapters is a live vendor call.

The source of truth for Santa Cruz County is the October 2026 business kit: the build brief, `county_santa_cruz.json`, the pilot filing guide, checklist, comp analysis, the appeal-letter template, and the customer guide. This product does not add fees, phone numbers, deadlines, or savings figures that are not in that kit or computed from numbers the visitor types.

## Journey

1. **Free check** (`index.html`). Address only. Email is asked after the result. The headline is “Think your property is assessed too high?” until the visitor types an address or opens the page with `?county=` set. Then the city in that headline comes from the address, or from Santa Cruz when `?county=santa-cruz` (including the `/santa-cruz/` page, which sets that parameter for the tab). An IP lookup may fill the state. It never puts an IP city in the headline, and a city alone does not choose the launch or non-launch screen. A county name from the lookup can. `?county=santa-cruz` overrides the lookup, a saved choice, and the typed address for that tab. After an address is typed, that address’s city or county overrides the IP result. Next to the check, the page says “Showing results for Santa Cruz County · change” when that county is selected. The same control reads “your county” until a county is known. Under the check: “Free check. No account needed to see your result. Results are estimates.” If the place is still ambiguous, the page shows the county picker and does not show prices. Santa Cruz County shows the $79 line with “+ $50 county fee, paid when you file” and “$249, county fee included,” plus the button “Check my property”. The $50 is `filing_fee.amount` on that adapter. The fee is non-refundable, and the adapter notes that fee waivers exist. Any other confirmed county shows a reminder signup and no prices.
2. **Numbers** (`values.html`). County, assessed value, the visitor's market-value estimate, optional tax bill, confidence, and an optional supplemental notice date. The market field's helper text is "Not sure? Use your recent purchase price or skip." Skipping does not block the check.
3. **Result** (`results.html`). One of four outcomes from `js/engine.js`. Email is collected here, with the privacy line, to save the result or to request a filing-window reminder. A verified open county can continue to checkout. An unconfirmed county, a paper-only county, and a closed window get a reminder signup instead of We file it. Los Angeles can buy the DIY kit only, until `agent.registration_status` is `active`. A closed window also points at the adapter's free informal review.
4. **Account** (`login.html`). Magic link, simulated. The link is shown on the page because no email is sent. Open it in the same tab.
5. **Plan and pay** (`checkout.html`). Exactly two cards when the county may sell them: DIY kit $79 and We file it $249. When the county adapter's `filing_fee` amount is above zero, the DIY card price is one line, “$79 + $50 county fee, paid when you file” (the amount comes from that field) and the We file it card reads “$249, county fee included.” A `filing_fee` of 0 or missing does not say the county fee is included. `SHOW_199_PLUS_COUNTY_FEE` in `js/config.js` is off. Turning it on switches the We file it line to “$199 + county fee” (or “$199 + $50 county fee” when the amount is known). Each card shows the price next to the estimated tax difference, labeled "Estimate from your numbers, if the county agrees to your value." If no tax bill was entered, the card says no dollar estimate is available. Contingency is not a checkout plan. While the window is open, the plan step shows “County filing deadline: Nov 30, 2026” from `regular_window.end`, plus “We file it orders close Nov 25” and a live countdown to the end of November 25. `order_cutoff` in `js/config.js` is that date for Santa Cruz. Every other county closes We file it orders 5 days before `regular_window.end`. We file it checkout closes after that cutoff day. The DIY kit stays open through the county deadline. Both cards save a card (a simulated SetupIntent, status `card_saved`) and do not charge it. The We file it card says “We'll save your card today. You won't be charged until the county confirms your appeal is filed.” The $79 card says “Card saved. Not charged while payment timing is under attorney review.” and “[DRAFT – payment timing pending attorney review]”. The $79 card stays on hold. The $49 monitor saves a card the same way and does not charge it. Ops “Mark filed, then charge” is the capture. The button is shown only when the appeal already has a filing time, a filing ID, and `countyConfirmedAt`. The click checks those again before charging and does not set `countyConfirmedAt`. Choosing We file it shows a required acknowledgment checkbox that starts unchecked. The checkbox is the sentence “We prepare your evidence, file your appeal, and work toward an informal reduction or settlement with the Assessor. If your case goes to a hearing, you attend (in person or by video where the county allows) using a hearing packet we prepare. If no one attends, the board will deny the appeal.” DIY does not show that checkbox. Checkout does not say “pay now” or give a payment due date. We file it is hidden for an unconfirmed county, a paper county (`wet_signature_required`), Los Angeles until registration is active, and the last 3 days of an e-file window (7 days for paper). A closed window stops checkout, points at the free informal review, and offers a reminder. If the window has ended, the screen says filing closed on that end date, the next regular window opens July 2 of the following year, and that year's close date is not listed. No outcome is promised. A start that is still ahead is quoted from the file. If the file has no dates either way, it says this county file does not list a future filing window and that no opening date is promised.
6. **Intake** (`intake.html`). Property confirm, occupancy, exemption question, condition notes, optional photo/PDF upload, and the authorization checkboxes. Los Angeles, San Francisco, Alameda, Santa Cruz, San Bernardino, and Sonoma also ask whether the customer wants to ask the county about a fee waiver. The question is driven by `fee_waiver.available`. When `fee_waiver.url` is set, the question links to that county page and does not restate who qualifies. Santa Cruz links to the county appeals site. San Francisco links to the county fees page. Los Angeles, Alameda, San Bernardino, and Sonoma still have no criteria URL in the county file, so the question does not invent one.
7. **Kit** (`kit.html`). Short explanation (150 characters where the adapter says so) and a Statement of Facts preview. The visitor approves it.
8. **Signing** (`sign.html`). Round 1 is the county's agent authorization when the plan files as agent. Before that round, a plain-language explainer says the named agent signs filings only, states the hearing sentence (you attend; if no one attends, the board will deny the appeal), says why the county needs the authorization (from `agent_authorization` on the adapter), and “How to revoke:” plus `agent_authorization.revocation` when the adapter has that string. Otherwise the line stays “How to revoke: [pending verification]”. Santa Cruz Local Rule 6 offers two written-authorization choices: the application authorization section, or the Santa Cruz County Agent Authorization form. The signed record stores which one was chosen. The authorization summary (state rule 305) names the parcel (address and APN), the tax years covered (the filing year and earlier years, four at most, with no future year), the applicant's signature and title, and the sentence “You get a copy of everything filed.” The date signed is the day the signature is completed. There is no date field and no backdating. Agent name, address, and phone stay `[pending]`. Filing stays off if any of those fields is empty or is the literal `[pending]`, if Santa Cruz is missing the Local Rule 6 choice, or if `signedAt` is after the filing time. Round 2 is the application certification plus the statement. Round 3 only if the visitor accepts a stipulation preview later.
9. **Filing** (`filed.html`). We file it does not submit on its own. The filing button stays disabled, with an explanation, until the signed agent authorization is stored on the matter as `agentAuthorization` and the agent name, address, and phone are real details rather than the literal `[pending]`. DIY kit: the adapter's paper or portal checklist, and Sherwood does not submit.
10. **Tracking** (`dashboard.html`). Stages, expectations, notices, notification preferences, annual monitor. The hearing stage shows the same hearing sentence and does not offer a hearing-support add-on.
11. **Ops** (`admin.html`). Pipeline, deadline list, review queue. This browser only.

## Go / no-go

The engine does not look up the assessor and does not invent comparable sales.

- **Off-season** if today is outside the adapter's regular window, unless a supplemental notice date falls inside `supplemental_days` on that adapter. Santa Cruz: July 2–November 30, 2026, and 60 days from a supplemental notice. October 13, 2026 is inside that window, so the result can be Go and checkout stays open. This is a waiting state, not a dead end. If the window has ended, the page says filing closed on that end date, the next regular window opens July 2 of the following year, and that year's close date is not in the file. No outcome is promised. A start that is still ahead is quoted from the file. A passed window is not called the next window. A "Remind me when filing opens" signup is stored on the matter and in the local outbox. The closed screen also shows `informal_review` under “Free informal review.” Santa Cruz informal review is the Assessor's Prop 8 request. There is no purchase path into checkout.
- **No-go** if a market-value estimate was entered and it is not below the assessed value.
- **Borderline → human review** if market value was skipped, the county file is unconfirmed or otherwise not verified, the window's start date is missing, the visitor marked the value as a guess, estimated savings are not above the adapter's filing fee, or (when no tax bill was entered) the gap is under 5% of assessed value. Skipping market value does not invent a reduction or a dollar estimate. The 5% line is Sherwood's review rule, not a legal standard and not a county rule. Borderline matters sit in the ops queue until "Mark reviewed." A closed window wins over a skip: the result is off-season, not borderline. An unconfirmed county does not get a We file it purchase. Los Angeles is the exception for the DIY kit only.
- **Go** otherwise, when the adapter is verified and the window is open.

Savings, when a tax bill was entered: `(assessed − market estimate) × (tax bill ÷ assessed)`. Labeled an estimate. The pilot property's 1.23% effective rate is not applied to anyone else's home. That 1.23% and the ~$1,955 figure stay on the case study, where they belong to that one filing.

## Post-filing timeline

The dashboard stages are a product choice, not a county status feed:

Filed → County acknowledged → Assessor review → Hearing scheduled → Decision → Corrected bill / refund → Annual monitor.

Why this shape: the build brief asks for notice tracking through hearing, decision, and refund, and for an annual monitor. The kit also says the assessor can signal a higher value and that withdrawal rules exist, but it has no stipulation form. Assessor review is still on the timeline because a value discussion can happen before a hearing. Accept and decline are the visitor's decision, in plain language, with no recommendation. Accepting opens signing round 3, which is explicitly a product step (`product_step` on the adapter) and not a county form. Declining leaves the hearing path.

Each stage's "what to expect" sentences are `stage_expectations` on the county adapter. Santa Cruz includes: keep paying taxes, up to two years to decide, hearing notice at least 45 days ahead, 3 copies of the statement unless the clerk wants more, the board can raise or lower or leave the value, the decision is final with superior-court review, and a proportionate refund if a reduction is granted.

The **Advance one stage** control is a demo toggle. It does not contact the county. A hearing preview date is set only when the adapter has `notice_minimum_days` (45 for Santa Cruz). No date is invented when that field is null.

The notices inbox stores pasted mail and runs a keyword match (`hearing`, `stipulation`, `decision`, `refund`). It does not parse a real notice. Email and SMS preferences write to a local outbox. Messages are not sent. The build brief's 60/30/7-day reminder idea is named on the dashboard and not implemented as a scheduler.

No one from Sherwood attends a hearing. The hearing screen states that Sherwood prepares the evidence, files the appeal, and works toward an informal reduction or settlement with the Assessor. If the case goes to a hearing, the owner attends, in person or by video where the county allows, using a hearing packet Sherwood prepares. If no one attends, the board will deny the appeal. There is no hearing-support add-on and no hearing price. Annual monitor enrolls at the published $49/yr in the same simulated payment adapter.

## Decisions

- **Static demo, one browser.** A `sherwood_db_v1` object in `localStorage` holds users, matters, payments, envelopes, filings, and the outbox. The signed-in user and the current matter id are in `sessionStorage`. There is no account server. The ops page can only see this browser.
- **County copy is data.** Marketing pages that used to type the Santa Cruz phone number now fill it from the adapter. The case study still tells the pilot story, including that one home's published figures ($158,915 reduction sought, about $1,955 a year, filed in a day, awaiting a decision).
- **No invented comps.** The free check blurs a sample line and says sales were not pulled. The statement preview says the same.
- **You sign the certification.** Round 2 is required for every checkout plan. Round 1 is required only for We file it (plan id `dfy`). The document title, source, and verified flag come from `agent_authorization` on the county adapter. The signed record is `matter.agentAuthorization` (`doc_title`, `source`, `verified`, `signerName`, `signerTitle`, `signedAt`, `envelopeId`, `parcel`, `taxYears`, `ownerGetsCopy`, and the agent name, address, and phone). The named agent stays on that summary and signs filings only. `signedAt` is the completion time. The form has no date control. Agent name, address, and phone are `[pending]`. The filing button stays disabled while any of those fields is empty or is the literal `[pending]`, and while `signedAt` is after the filing time. Revocation steps are not invented. The explainer uses `agent_authorization.revocation` when the adapter has it, and otherwise "How to revoke: [pending verification]".
- **Two checkout plans.** DIY kit $79 and We file it $249. Santa Cruz `filing_fee.amount` is 50 and `refundable` is false, so the DIY price is one line, “$79 + $50 county fee, paid when you file”, and We file it reads “$249, county fee included.” The adapter note says the county fee is non-refundable and that fee waivers exist. The $249 card says “We'll save your card today. You won't be charged until the county confirms your appeal is filed.” The $79 card says “Card saved. Not charged while payment timing is under attorney review.” and “[DRAFT – payment timing pending attorney review]”. The $79 card stays on hold. `SHOW_199_PLUS_COUNTY_FEE` is false. The same price lines are on the pricing page, the FAQ, the home launch line, and the filing confirmation. A missing or zero `filing_fee` hides every county-fee line. The comparison label on each card is "Estimate from your numbers, if the county agrees to your value." No outcome is promised. Contingency stays on the pricing page as "Coming soon, pending review." There is no hearing-support row and no $149 hearing price. The We file it fee terms include the hearing sentence. Checkout stores `hearingAcknowledgment` only after that checkbox is checked for We file it.
- **Closed window is a hard stop.** `regular_window` carries `source_url` and `verified_date`. Checkout cannot proceed when `filingWindow` is not `open`, including a window that has closed and a window whose start is missing. If the window has ended, `nextWindowCopy` says filing closed on that end date and that the next regular window opens July 2 of the following year, without that year's close date and without a promised outcome. A start that is still ahead is quoted from the file. A passed window is not called the next window. The off-season result does not link into checkout. On an open window the plan step shows `deadlineBadge`, “County filing deadline: Nov 30, 2026” for Santa Cruz, plus “We file it orders close Nov 25” and a countdown that is computed in the browser until the end of that day. `order_cutoff` is `2026-11-25` for Santa Cruz. Every other county closes We file it orders 5 days before `regular_window.end`. The cutoff day is the last day a We file it order is accepted. The DIY kit stays available through the county deadline. Emails print “County filing deadline: Nov 30, 2026”, or a day count computed from the send date. They do not bake in a remaining-day number.
- **Skip market value.** Empty market value, or the Skip control, is borderline human review when the window is open. It does not block, and it does not invent a statistic.
- **Borderline does not auto-file.** Ops has to mark it reviewed. The demo path in the section below is a Go, so it does not stop there.
- **Off-season still keeps the lead.** "Remind me when filing opens" sets `remindWhenOpen` on the matter. The $49 monitor can be recorded from that same screen.
- **Refund policy is a draft.** `trust.html` and the pricing page mark the $249 terms “[DRAFT – pending attorney review]”. The card is saved and not charged until the county confirms the filing. Cancel before that confirmation: no charge. Cancel after that charge: refund minus the county fee. County rejects because of our error: we refile or give a full refund. County rejects because of the customer's information: refund our service portion only. This is not a final policy.
- **Room 520 vs Room 500** stays on the Santa Cruz adapter as an address conflict. The product shows Room 520. It does not quote a $30 paper fee.

## Demo path

Use the verified Santa Cruz adapter while its window is open (through November 30, 2026). Any assessed value clearly above your market estimate works. To mirror the published pilot math, the bill figures from that case study are an assessed value of $1,233,915 and a market estimate of $1,075,000. Add a tax bill only if you want a dollar savings estimate; the pilot's own tax was $15,176.44, which is how that case produced about $1,955. You do not have to use those numbers.

1. Open the site and submit an address and email.
2. On the numbers screen choose **Santa Cruz — Verified from the October 12, 2026 pilot kit**. Enter an assessed value above the market estimate. Leave confidence on "Sure enough to use." Submit.
3. The result should say **Go**. Continue.
4. On sign-in, create the link and open **Open the simulated magic link** in the same tab.
5. Choose **We file it** (“$249, county fee included.”). Check the hearing acknowledgment. It starts unchecked and blocks continue until it is checked. The card is saved with status `card_saved` and not charged. The page says “We'll save your card today. You won't be charged until the county confirms your appeal is filed.” The other card is **DIY kit** (“$79 + $50 county fee, paid when you file”) with “Card saved. Not charged while payment timing is under attorney review.” and “[DRAFT – payment timing pending attorney review]”. While the window is open the page shows “County filing deadline: Nov 30, 2026”, “We file it orders close Nov 25”, and a live countdown to the end of November 25. No card number is requested. Nothing is marked paid. Contingency is not on this screen. Each card shows the price against the estimate, with the label "Estimate from your numbers, if the county agrees to your value."
6. Complete intake. Photos are optional.
7. Approve the kit.
8. Round 1 starts with the explainer (the named agent signs filings only, the hearing sentence, why Santa Cruz needs it, the two Local Rule 6 choices, and the adapter's revoke line). The summary still names the agent. Continue, then sign the agent authorization: choose the application authorization section or the county Agent Authorization form, pick up to four tax years, type your name, and type a title. Sign round 2 (certification and statement). The signing screens say they are not DocuSign.
9. The filing screen keeps **Record the filing** disabled while agent name, address, or phone is the literal `[pending]`. Those placeholders are not a completed Rule 305 authorization, so the button does not file. The portal is not contacted. The page was not filed on load.
10. Open **Track the appeal**. Read "What to expect now." Use **Advance one stage** to walk acknowledged → assessor review. Accept (round 3) or decline, then advance through hearing, decision, refund, and monitor. Enroll the monitor if you want the $49 record.
11. Paste a sentence containing "hearing" into Notices and save. The keyword note should suggest the hearing stage.
12. Open **Ops** (`admin.html`) and see the matter in the pipeline and Santa Cruz on the deadline list.

To see the other outcomes: a market estimate at or above the assessed value is a no-go; leaving market value blank, pressing Skip, marking a rough guess, or a gap under 5% with no tax bill is borderline; any of the September 15 counties (Alameda, for example) is off-season on October 13, 2026. That result says filing closed September 15, 2026, the next regular window opens July 2, 2027, and no close date or outcome is promised. It points at the free informal review and offers a reminder. It does not sell We file it. An unconfirmed county whose window is still open, such as San Diego, offers a reminder only. Los Angeles offers the DIY kit and hides We file it.

## Open legal questions

From the build brief, section 9. The pilot did not answer these. They need a California property-tax attorney before a real launch.

- Unauthorized practice of law: where form preparation stops and legal advice starts. Customer copy on this site is written to stay on the form-preparation side and to say Sherwood is not a law firm.
- Whether a non-attorney agent may represent a homeowner at an Assessment Appeals Board hearing in each target county, and any registration or qualification rules. Santa Cruz Rule 6, as cited in the brief, covers filing by an authorized agent. This product does not send anyone to the hearing. The named agent signs filings only. The owner attends if the case goes to a hearing.
- Contingency-fee compliance for non-attorney tax representatives. The 35% idea is not sold at checkout. The pricing page says "Coming soon, pending review." That is not a legal opinion that the fee is allowed.
- Data licensing for MLS or comp data at commercial scale.
- Privacy and CCPA handling of homeowner personal information. This demo avoids a server by keeping data in the browser, which is not a CCPA program.

## Per-county variation

A new county is a new JSON file plus a line in `data/counties/index.json`, then a verified test filing before `status` may be `verified`. The UI already branches on:

- `filing.efile` and `portal_url` (submit vs paper steps)
- `filing.filing_fee` (a number, or `{ amount, refundable, note }`; an amount above zero is the county fee on the plan cards; 0 or missing hides that line)
- `efile_fee.amount` / `paper_fee.amount` (null means "do not quote a number"; these stay as kit facts)
- `regular_window.start` / `end`, plus `source_url` and `verified_date` (null until someone verifies that county)
- `supplemental_days`
- `agent_authorization.doc_title`, `source`, and `verified`
- `signature_steps[].required_for`
- `agent.agent_filing_allowed` (null on templates)
- `hearing.format`, `notice_minimum_days`, `prep`
- `stage_expectations`, `rules`, `timelines`
- `homeowner_exemption` (omitted means the intake will not quote $7,000 or $86)

Until `status` is `verified`, the engine will not return Go.

## Place, email, and privacy

The first screen does not ask for an email. `js/place.js` decides the county in this order: the `?county=` parameter (kept for the browser tab), a property address the visitor typed, a county they picked in “Showing results for”, then an IP lookup that uses a county name only. An IP city such as Cupertino does not choose a variant and is not written into the headline. The headline city is filled only from a typed address or from `?county=`. The state may still fill `data-geo="region"`.

The first landing URL that carries `src` or `utm_*` is stored once in this browser (`sherwood_attribution`) and copied onto a reminder, a saved result, and the appeal. A later link does not replace it. An empty first visit does not block a later campaign. `?county=` stays separate, so `?county=santa-cruz&src=mail1` still selects the county. ## Reminders backend

`backend/` is the Firebase project: rules, indexes, and Node 20 functions. `js/config.js` holds `FUNCTIONS_BASE_URL`. While that string is empty, reminder and result emails stay in this browser and no request is made.

When it is set, the site POSTs to `createReminder` (outside a launch county, or when the filing window is closed) and `createLead` (email me this result). Checkout can POST `markConverted`. The function stores marketing consent with `consent_source`, `consent_requested_at`, and `confirmed_at`. `confirmed_at` is set only from the double opt-in link. A daily job in America/Los_Angeles sends a heads-up during the 14 days before the adapter opens, an open-window note, and a deadline nudge during the 14 days before the adapter deadline if the reminder is confirmed and not converted. The email prints “County filing deadline: Nov 30, 2026” from the adapter end date, or a day count computed when the message is sent. It does not hardcode how many days remain. Dates come from the county adapter. Mail goes out through Resend. It is not sent unless `MAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS`, `MAIL_FROM`, `RESEND_API_KEY`, and `PUBLIC_FUNCTIONS_BASE_URL` are set. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, that postal address, and the B&P 17537.9 disclosure as the first block.

Setup steps for the owner are in `backend/README.md`. The GitHub workflow deploys rules and functions on a push to `main` that touches `backend/`, and it skips when `FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` or `FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID` is missing.
