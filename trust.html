Disclosures

This assessment reduction filing service is not associated with any government agency. If you disagree with the assessed value of your property, you have the right to an informal assessment review, at no cost, by contacting the assessor's office directly. If you and the assessor cannot agree to the value of the property or if you do not wish to contact the assessor you can obtain and file an application for changed assessment with the county board of equalization or assessment appeals board on your own behalf. An appeals board has the authority to raise property values (but in no case higher than the Proposition 13 protected value) as well as to lower property values.

How we protect data, on this demo

  • Your check, intake, files, and simulated payment record are stored in this browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to Sherwood.
  • There is no card field. The payment button does not collect a card number.
  • Photos you attach stay in this browser. Skip anything you would not want sitting in local storage. Large files are skipped.
  • A production service would need a real privacy review. The build brief lists CCPA handling of homeowner information as an open question. This page does not claim that review is done.

What you do

  • You supply the address, the assessed value, and your market-value estimate.
  • You confirm you are an owner, or otherwise entitled to file.
  • You review the kit.
  • You sign the agent authorization if Sherwood is going to file.
  • You sign the application certification. Sherwood does not sign it as you.
  • You keep paying your property taxes on time.
  • You decide whether to accept a stipulation if one is ever offered. Sherwood does not choose for you.
  • We prepare a hearing packet for you. You attend, or the appeal is denied.

What Sherwood does

  • Reads the county adapter for the deadline, fee, portal or paper steps, clerk, and rules.
  • Computes an estimate from the numbers you entered.
  • Prepares a Statement of Facts preview from your intake. Comparable sales are not invented.
  • On We file it, the named agent signs filings only, after you sign the authorization document on that county's adapter. The filing button stays off until that signature is stored on the appeal.
  • Tracks the stages you see on your appeal page, including notices you paste in.

What Sherwood does not do

  • Give legal advice, or tell you the board will lower the value.
  • Sign a perjury certification in your name.
  • Attend a hearing for you.
  • Guarantee a reduction, a refund, or a timeline beyond what the adapter already says.
  • Quote a fee, a phone number, or a deadline that is not in the county adapter.
