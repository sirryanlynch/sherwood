How it works

You go from “I wonder if I'm overpaying” to a filing packet you have reviewed. Sherwood is not a law firm. You sign anything that has to be signed by the owner.

  1. Free check

    Address and email. Then the assessed value, your market-value estimate, and the county. That county's adapter supplies the window and the fee.

  2. A clear result

    Go, borderline (a person reviews), no-go with a reason, or off-season (waitlist, and the adapter's note on asking the assessor). Savings, when shown, are computed from your tax bill. We do not borrow the pilot property's tax rate.

  3. Account and plan

    A simulated magic link, then DIY ($79), done-for-you ($249), or contingency (35% of first-year savings, nothing due today). The county fee is extra and comes from the adapter.

  4. Intake

    Confirm the property and APN, occupancy, Homeowner's Exemption if that adapter has the facts, condition notes, and optional photos stored only in this browser.

  5. Kit, then your signature

    You review a Statement of Facts built from what you entered. Comparable sales are not invented. You sign an agent authorization if Sherwood is filing, then you sign the certification. Sherwood does not sign that certification.

  6. Filing

    Done-for-you and contingency: a simulated portal confirmation, with the portal link, fee, and clerk from the adapter. DIY: the adapter's own checklist, and you file.

  7. After filing

    A timeline from filed through refund, plus an annual-monitor option at $49/yr. You can paste county mail into the notices inbox. Keep paying your taxes.
