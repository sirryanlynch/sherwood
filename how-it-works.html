Disclosures

This assessment reduction filing service is not associated with any government agency. If you disagree with the assessed value of your property, you have the right to an informal assessment review, at no cost, by contacting the assessor's office directly. If you and the assessor cannot agree to the value of the property or if you do not wish to contact the assessor you can obtain and file an application for changed assessment with the county board of equalization or assessment appeals board on your own behalf. An appeals board has the authority to raise property values (but in no case higher than the Proposition 13 protected value) as well as to lower property values.

The path

One filing, start to finish

01

Free check

Address, then the assessed value, your market-value estimate, and the county. Email is asked after the result. That county's adapter supplies the window and the fee.

02

Estimate

Go, borderline (a person reviews, including when you skip market value), no-go with a reason, or a closed window. A closed window offers a reminder for the adapter's opening date and the $49 annual monitor. Savings, when shown, are computed from your tax bill. We do not borrow the pilot property's tax rate.

03

We file

A simulated magic link, then We file it ($249, county fee included). Checkout stops when that county's filing window is closed.

You confirm the property, add condition notes, and sign the certification yourself. Comparable sales are not invented. Sherwood does not sign that certification.

04

Confirmation

The filing button stays off until that county's agent authorization is signed and stored. Then a simulated confirmation, with the portal link, fee, and clerk from the adapter. The drawing's mark is the lower value the filing seeks, not a decision. Keep paying your taxes.
