How it works

You go from “I wonder if I'm overpaying” to a filed appeal in one sitting.

  1. Enter your address

    Address and email. That's all the free check needs.

  2. We run the analysis

    We pull your assessed value from the county assessor and find comparable home sales near the January 1 lien date. From those we form an opinion of market value and estimate your savings.

  3. See your results

    “You're likely over-assessed by about $X/yr.” Comparable addresses and prices stay hidden until you unlock the full report.

  4. Choose a plan and complete intake

    Confirm the property, tell us about owner-occupancy and condition (roof age, damage, remodels, systems), add your contact details, and sign an agent authorization if we're filing for you.

  5. We build your kit

    Prefilled application values, a comparable-sales table, a Statement of Facts PDF, and a filing checklist.

  6. Filing

    We file through your county's assessment appeals portal as your authorized agent. You e-sign the certification yourself; we never sign it for you. Example from our launch county (Santa Cruz County, CA): filing uses the Assessment Appeals e-file portal, and the county charges a $50 filing fee (2026 portal rate).

  7. Notice tracking

    We watch for hearing notices, decisions, and refund bills, and tell you at each step. You'll get a hearing notice at least 45 days ahead. The board can take up to two years to decide.

  8. Decision

    If your assessment is reduced, we confirm your actual savings. Contingency fees are billed only on a win.
