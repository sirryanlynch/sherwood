How it works

You go from a free check to a filing packet you have reviewed. Sherwood is not a law firm. You sign anything that has to be signed by the owner. Results are estimates.

  1. Free check

    Address, then the assessed value, your market-value estimate, and the county. Email is asked after the result. That county's adapter supplies the window and the fee.

  2. A clear result

    Go, borderline (a person reviews, including when you skip market value), no-go with a reason, or a closed window. A closed window offers a reminder for the adapter's opening date and the $49 annual monitor. Savings, when shown, are computed from your tax bill. We do not borrow the pilot property's tax rate.

  3. Account and plan

    A simulated magic link, then two plans: DIY kit ($79 + $50 county fee, paid when you file) or We file it ($249, county fee included). Checkout stops when that county's filing window is closed.

  4. Intake

    Confirm the property and APN, occupancy, Homeowner's Exemption if that adapter has the facts, condition notes, and optional photos stored only in this browser.

  5. Kit, then your signature

    You review a Statement of Facts built from what you entered. Comparable sales are not invented. You sign an agent authorization if Sherwood is filing, then you sign the certification. Sherwood does not sign that certification.

  6. Filing

    We file it: the filing button stays off until that county's agent authorization is signed and stored on the appeal. Then a simulated confirmation, with the portal link, fee, and clerk from the adapter. DIY kit: the adapter's own checklist, and you file.

  7. After filing

    A timeline from filed through refund, plus an annual-monitor option at $49/yr. You can paste county mail into the notices inbox. Keep paying your taxes.
