This assessment reduction filing service is not associated with any government agency. If you disagree with the assessed value of your property, you have the right to an informal assessment review, at no cost, by contacting the assessor's office directly. If you and the assessor cannot agree to the value of the property or if you do not wish to contact the assessor you can obtain and file an application for changed assessment with the county board of equalization or assessment appeals board on your own behalf. An appeals board has the authority to raise property values (but in no case higher than the Proposition 13 protected value) as well as to lower property values.

Questions

When is the deadline?

It depends on the county adapter. The launch county's window, fee, and clerk are loaded below from that file, not typed into this page. Other county files on this site are unconfirmed.

Do I still have to pay my property taxes?

Yes. Filing does not pause the bill. If a reduction is granted, the launch-county rules say a proportionate refund of taxes paid is issued. Your adapter's rules are listed with your result.

Who signs?

You do. The perjury certification is yours. Sherwood never signs it as you. If you choose We file it, you also sign the agent authorization defined on that county's adapter. Filing stays off until that signature is stored on the appeal. The signing screen says how to revoke is pending verification.

Is this legal advice?

No. Sherwood is not a law firm. We prepare materials and, with your authorization, file as your agent. For legal questions, talk to an attorney. The build brief lists open questions that still need a California property-tax attorney.

What if my county is not listed as verified?

You can still run the check. An unconfirmed county file never comes back as an automatic Go, and Sherwood will not create a filing confirmation from it. We'll keep the lead.

What do the plans cost?

DIY kit $79 + $50 county fee, paid when you file. We file it $249, county fee included. Those lines are filled from the launch-county adapter.

Can the board raise my assessment?

Where the adapter says so, yes. The launch-county form instructions say the board can increase, decrease, or leave the assessment unchanged. That risk is on the result page for whatever county you picked, taken from that adapter's rules.
