Disclosures

This assessment reduction filing service is not associated with any government agency. If you disagree with the assessed value of your property, you have the right to an informal assessment review, at no cost, by contacting the assessor's office directly. If you and the assessor cannot agree to the value of the property or if you do not wish to contact the assessor you can obtain and file an application for changed assessment with the county board of equalization or assessment appeals board on your own behalf. An appeals board has the authority to raise property values (but in no case higher than the Proposition 13 protected value) as well as to lower property values.

Questions

When is the deadline?

It depends on the county. The launch county's window, fee, and clerk are loaded below from that file, not typed into this page. Other county files on this site are unconfirmed.

Do I still have to pay my property taxes?

Yes. Keep paying your property taxes on time. An appeal does not pause your bill. If a reduction is granted, this county's details say a proportionate refund of taxes paid is issued. Your county's details are listed with your result.

Who signs?

You do. The perjury certification is yours. Sherwood never signs it as you. If you choose We file it, you also sign the agent authorization defined in your county's details. Filing stays off until that signature is stored on the appeal. The signing screen says how to revoke is pending verification.

Is this legal advice?

No. Sherwood is not a law firm. We prepare materials and, with your authorization, file as your agent. For legal questions, talk to an attorney. The build brief lists open questions that still need a property-tax attorney.

Isn't this a scam? The county does this for free.

Yes, you can file yourself for free, and we say so. We're for people who'd rather not.

What if I pay and it fails?

You pay $249 to have it prepared and filed. We can't promise an outcome. We only recommend filing when the math clears the fee. A refund tied to the appeal's outcome is not offered. That would work like a contingency fee, and contingency is off pending attorney review.

Do I have to go to a hearing?

Possibly. If there's a hearing, you attend. We give you the packet. We prepare a hearing packet for you. You attend, or the appeal is denied.

What happens after I pay?

You get an email at each step: received, signed, filed (PDFs attached), hearing scheduled, and decision. Your case page keeps the signed authorization, the filed application, the county confirmation, key dates, and whose move is next. If we receive a hearing notice, we ask you to confirm you got it. The case page does not promise proactive status updates.

Will you negotiate with the assessor?

We prepare your evidence, file your appeal, and work toward an informal reduction or settlement with the Assessor. If your case goes to a hearing, you attend. If the assessor offers a settlement, you decide whether to accept it.

Why $249?

It's flat, and the county fee is included in Santa Cruz County. No percentage of your savings.

What if my county is not listed as verified?

You can still run the check. An unconfirmed county file never comes back as an automatic Go, and Sherwood will not create a filing confirmation from it. We'll keep the lead.

What do the plans cost?

We file it $249, county fee included. That line is filled from your county's details.

Can the board raise my assessment?

Where your county's details say so, yes. This county's details say the board can increase, decrease, or leave the assessment unchanged. That risk is on the result page for whatever county you picked, taken from your county's details.
